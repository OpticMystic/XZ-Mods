Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard on the game screen. Easy keeps the air angle you set. A / D or drag: steer. Shift + A / D: fast spin, hops off the ground. Press the other way to slow a spin. Land along the hill. Three perfects in a row light the rocket. Space: dive. P: pause.
