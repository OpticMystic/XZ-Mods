Wave Rider · A game by VJ OpticMystic

Actual C physics and renderer, using exported waveform data. Simulated playhead; no audio or device control.

Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard on the control-check screen. Easy holds the air angle you set.

Player 1 A / D tilt · Hold Q to add weight (CUE) · R quick spin

Player 2 ← / → tilt · Hold Enter to add weight (CUE) · / quick spin

Spin Lift is optional; turn it on or off on the last setup screen. With it on, press R or / once as you near a rising crest. That sends one quick spin, like a fast jog turn, and arms a one-time extra lift. You still have to release weight and let the hill launch you. These keys only test the timing; they don't reproduce how the jog feels.

Tilt is bounded: the nose stops at its limit and never spins. Holding Q or Enter is the same as holding CUE: it adds weight to dive down slopes. Release to soar off the crests. The mouse wheel tilts; holding either side of the screen adds weight. Keys 5 / 6 use earned powers. Space pauses the preview.
