SCREEN is the MODS section for how the player looks. Here you choose between the MODERN and CLASSIC interfaces, pick a theme, set the waveform colours and the 3-band colours, and decide how keys are written. Nothing on this page changes how a track sounds.

Open MODS with [[MENU]], then choose **SCREEN** in the section rail.

## MODERN and CLASSIC

XZ Mods gives the player two interfaces. You choose one with **INTERFACE** on the SCREEN page, or when you prepare the USB in the XZ Mods app. MODERN is the default.

- **MODERN** is a full-screen layout drawn by XZ Mods, with bigger waveforms and a browse sidebar.
- **CLASSIC** keeps the player's own play screen and adds MODS to it. The classic themes restyle that screen.

| | MODERN | CLASSIC |
|---|---|---|
| Play screen | Drawn by XZ Mods, full screen | The XDJ-XZ's own layout, restyled |
| Themes | One look, STANDARD. MODS is drawn in FLAGSHIP | 24 themes. MODS is drawn in the theme you pick |
| Waveform colours | STOCK, RGB, 3-BAND, STEMS | STOCK, 3-BAND, STEMS |
| Lamps and titles | Each part can be shown or hidden | Not available |
| Playing key | Shown as the big key in each deck's column | Shown where the beat countdown sits |
| Stem buttons | Six places, including small buttons beside the hot cues | Five places |
| Browse | The XZ Mods browser with a sidebar. See [Browsing and loading](browse) | The player's own browser |

Each interface keeps its own theme. If you switch from CLASSIC to MODERN and back, your classic theme comes back with you.

To switch without opening MODS, press the rekordbox button above **LINK**. Out of the box it switches between MODERN and CLASSIC. You can give it another job in **PLAY** › **REKORDBOX BUTTON**. See [Controls on the player](hardware).

## Classic themes {classic}

A classic theme restyles the player's own play screen and the MODS menu: artwork, text, waveforms, cue marks and dialogs. Track positions, numbers and touch areas stay where the player puts them, so a theme never moves a control under your finger.

Choose **THEME** on the SCREEN page to open **CLASSIC THEMES**. Tap a theme, or turn the encoder to it and push. Close MODS to see the play screen in the new theme.

## MODERN look {modern}

MODERN has one look, **STANDARD**, so it has no theme list. The MODS menu is drawn in FLAGSHIP: black pages, a grey section rail and blue selections. More MODERN looks are planned.

Instead of THEME, the SCREEN page offers **LAMPS AND TITLES**. Its row shows **ALL SHOWN**, or how many parts you have hidden.

| Group | Switch or choice | Shown | Hidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOP BAR | MODE LAMPS | The SINGLE / CONTINUE, A. HOT CUE and A. CUE lamps light while their mode is on | The lamps leave the screen. The modes keep working |
| TOP BAR | QUANTIZE LAMP | Each deck's QUANTIZE lamp and beat value above its time | The QUANTIZE button's own light still shows it |
| WAVEFORMS | TITLES IN WAVEFORMS | Track titles inside the waveform lanes | The waveforms use that space. Titles at the bottom stay |
| TRACK COLOURS | DECK + WAVE | The colours set for the USB in rekordbox show on the deck tag and its waveform | |
| TRACK COLOURS | DECK ONLY | Those colours show on the deck tag only | |
| TRACK COLOURS | OFF | USB colours are not shown | |

A hidden lamp also stops answering touch.

Your LAMPS AND TITLES choices, TRACK COLOURS and the **BYPASS BUTTON** switch are saved to the USB with your other settings, so they come back after the player restarts. So are MODERN's waveform colour, its waveform position and zoom, and how it writes keys.

## Waveform colours

**WAVEFORM COLOURS** sets how the scrolling waveforms are coloured. The setting itself is explained with the rest of the play screen in [The play screen](play-screen).

| Choice | What you see |
|---|---|
| STOCK | The player's own waveform colours |
| RGB | The full-colour waveform. MODERN only |
| 3-BAND | Lows, mids and highs as layers: lows outside, mids around them, highs at the core. Blue, amber and white unless you choose your own [3-band colours](#3-band-colours) |
| STEMS | Drums outside, harmonics around them, vocals at the core, in your stem colours |

- 3-BAND and STEMS read the analysis rekordbox and the XZ Mods stem builder already put on the USB. You do not prepare anything extra.
- A track without stems shows 3-BAND under STEMS. A track with four stems also shows 3-BAND, because its stems carry no waveform data yet.
- Stem colours are set in **STEMS** › **STEM COLOURS**. See [Stems](stems).
- In MODERN, UTILITY's **WAVEFORM COLOR** is the same setting. Your choice is saved to the USB and comes back after a restart.

## 3-band colours

**3-BAND COLOURS** chooses the colour of each band in the 3-BAND waveform: the lows, the mids and the highs. On the SCREEN page it sits beside **THEME** in CLASSIC and beside **LAMPS AND TITLES** in MODERN. Its row shows the three colours in use.

```figure classic:theme-{look}-band-colors MODS · 3-BAND COLOURS
Every band on STANDARD, the interface's own colours.
1 @ 212,63 LOWS | The colour of the lows, the outer layer of the waveform.
2 @ 432,63 MIDS | The colour of the mids, the middle layer.
3 @ 657,63 HIGHS | The colour of the highs, at the core.
4 @ 128,118 STANDARD | Keeps the interface's own colour for that band. The highlighted choice in each band is the colour in use.
5 @ 276,178 Colour choices | BLUE, RED, GREEN, AMBER, PURPLE, CYAN, PINK and WHITE. Tap one to give it to that band.
6 @ 470,298 Preview | A strip drawn the way the 3-BAND waveform stacks the bands, in the colours you chose.
7 @ 360,367 RESET 3-BAND COLOURS | Puts all three bands back to STANDARD.
8 @ 720,425 WORKS ON | 3-band colours apply on all four decks.
```

1. Open MODS > **SCREEN** > **3-BAND COLOURS**.
2. Tap a colour under **LOWS**, **MIDS** or **HIGHS**. Without the touch screen, turn the encoder to a colour and push.
3. Close MODS to see the play screen in the new colours.

| Band | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| LOWS | STANDARD, BLUE, RED, GREEN, AMBER, PURPLE, CYAN, PINK, WHITE | STANDARD |
| MIDS | The same nine | STANDARD |
| HIGHS | The same nine | STANDARD |

- The colours apply to every 3-BAND waveform: the MODERN lanes and overviews, the CLASSIC scrolling waveform, decks 3 and 4, and the browse preview.
- STANDARD is not one fixed colour. It is the colour the interface itself uses for that band, so MODERN and CLASSIC can differ a little.
- The 3-BAND pictures under **WAVEFORM COLOURS** on the SCREEN page are drawn in your colours too.
- Stem colours are separate. Set them in **STEMS** › **STEM COLOURS**.
- The colours are saved to the USB with your other settings.

## Keys

### Key notation

**KEY NOTATION** sets how keys are written everywhere MODS draws them: on the play screen, in the track list and in MODS.

| Choice | Example | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| AS ON THE USB | as set in your library | Keys look the way your rekordbox library writes them |
| MUSICAL | Am | You think in note names |
| WHEEL | 8A | You mix by key wheel numbers |

### Playing key

With **PLAYING KEY** on, each deck shows the key the track plays in now, after tempo changes and key shifts. The original key and the offset in cents sit beside it.

- In MODERN the playing key is the big key in each deck's column. Off shows the track's original key there.
- In CLASSIC the playing key sits where the beat countdown is. Off brings the countdown back.
- Decks 3 and 4 show their playing key too. Until a software deck reports it, the deck shows the track's own key, marked TRACK KEY in CLASSIC.

```gallery {modern}
modern:key-tempo-cents Deck 2 sped up with MT off. It still plays in Am, 38 cents sharp
modern:key-tempo-semitone Faster still. Deck 2 now plays in Bbm, and ORIGINAL shows Am
```

The key readout and Key Sync are explained in [The play screen](play-screen) and [Beat jump and performance features](performance).

### Key match colours and key before title

**KEY MATCH COLOURS** colours keys green or amber by how well they mix with the master deck, in the track list and on the play screen. Green means the key mixes with the master's key and sits within 25 cents of it. Amber means it would mix, but it is detuned by more than 25 cents. **KEY BEFORE TITLE** puts the key in front of each title in the track list. See [Browsing and loading](browse).

## Deck colours

When a screen names a deck, it draws the deck number in a small badge. The badge tells you the deck by its digit and the pair by its colours.

| Decks | Badge |
|---|---|
| 1 and 2 | White digit on grey |
| 3 and 4 | Black digit on white |
| A deck that is not live | The same badge, dimmed |

No deck owns a colour. Blue and pale blue mark the deck a side of the player controls. Amber, red, green, the waveform bands and USB colours keep their own meanings. The **WORKS ON** row in the MODS dock uses the same badges, in mixer order **3 1 2 4**. See [Four decks](four-decks) for the deck view.

## System readout

**SYSTEM READOUT** puts CPU load, memory and temperature beside **STEMS** and **MODS** on the top bar. Open it to see deck 3 and 4 audio render times and active key shifters across all four decks. P99 is the time at or below which 99% of the last 256 rendered blocks finished; MAX is the longest. Short blocks are scaled to a full block. D3+D4 MAX adds both maxima and compares them with the 5,805 microseconds available per block. New shifts use a stricter 3,483 microsecond budget, leaving time for other work. These render numbers include decoding, time stretch and key shift; they do not measure the complete output path. Use them when you suspect the player is working hard, for example with stems on both decks and four decks running.

```figure classic:theme-{look}-metrics-setup SYSTEM READOUT
The first setup step with live readings and four key shifters engaged.
1 @ 225,105 STEP | Where you are in the setup.
2 @ 440,206 What the numbers mean | CPU 100% means all four cores are busy. RAM leaves out cache. 80 C turns yellow, 90 C flashes red with HOT.
3 @ 430,255 Audio render time | Each software deck shows its p99 and maximum render time in microseconds. LAST RUN means the reading is no longer live.
4 @ 430,282 Key shifters | Counts shifted decks, including decks 1 and 2. The combined software maximum is shown against one audio block.
5 @ 700,356 DETECT METRICS | Runs the current step. Push to move through the setup.
```

1. Choose **SYSTEM READOUT** on the SCREEN page.
2. Push **DETECT METRICS**. The setup looks for CPU, memory and the temperature sensor.
3. Follow the remaining steps until the live readings show.

After setup the row on the SCREEN page reads **ON**. On the top bar, **METRICS STARTING - TAP TO SET UP** means the readings have not arrived yet. **METRICS STALE - TAP TO VERIFY** means they stopped updating. Tap either one to open the setup again.

> **Note.** Only the temperature of the main chip is available on the XDJ-XZ.

## When something looks wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The theme did not change | Close MODS. The play screen takes the new theme once MODS is closed |
| DECK VIEW is missing from SCREEN | This player's USB loader cannot run decks 3 and 4. See [Four decks](four-decks) |
| STEMS waveforms look like 3-BAND | The track has no prepared stems, or it has four stems. See [Stems](stems) |
| Keys show in the wrong notation | Set KEY NOTATION. AS ON THE USB follows your library's own setting |
| The waveform colours are not the ones you expect | Check 3-BAND COLOURS. RESET 3-BAND COLOURS puts every band back to STANDARD |
