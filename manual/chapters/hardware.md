XZ Mods leaves every control on the XDJ-XZ doing its stock job unless this page says otherwise. This page covers only the controls whose behaviour XZ Mods changes or adds, and the ones you use to drive MODS. For everything else, the XDJ-XZ operating instructions still apply.

## The button next to LINK

The XDJ-XZ has a media button directly above [[LINK]]. On a stock player it opens the library of a computer or phone connected to the player. XZ Mods gives it two slots: a plain press, and a press while [[SHIFT]] is held on either deck. Set them in MODS > PLAY > BUTTON NEXT TO LINK.

```figure classic:theme-{look}-buttons MODS · BUTTON NEXT TO LINK
Both slots at their defaults. A press switches the interface, and SHIFT + press keeps the button's own job.
1 @ 398,92 Media buttons | The four media buttons as they sit on the player, with the button next to LINK highlighted.
2 @ 185,92 PRESS | What a plain press does. The filled choice is the current one.
3 @ 420,204 What it does | One line explaining the current PRESS choice.
4 @ 225,228 SHIFT + PRESS | What the button does while SHIFT is held on either deck.
5 @ 535,340 What it does | One line explaining the current SHIFT + PRESS choice.
6 @ 190,425 Dock | Explains the highlighted choice before you pick it.
```

| Choice | What the button does |
|---|---|
| MODS MENU | Opens and closes MODS. MODS then works with the rotary selector and [[BACK]]. |
| ITS OWN JOB | The stock job: the screen for playing a computer's or phone's library. In MODERN the player's own screen for it takes over until you are back on the play screen. |
| INTERFACE | Switches between MODERN and CLASSIC, with the same notice as MODS > SCREEN. |
| VJ.TOOLS SCREEN | Switches the VJ.Tools screen on and off. Without an enabled, connected VJ.Tools link it says so instead. |
| STEMS | Steps the stem buttons on the play screen through their places, as the STEMS button on the screen does. |
| DECK VIEW | Steps DECK VIEW: 2 DECKS, 4 DECKS in number order 1 2 3 4, 4 DECKS in mixer order 3 1 2 4, then 2 DECKS again. It saves the choice and shows the new view in a short notice. |
| NOTHING | The button does nothing. |

> **Tip.** With the default INTERFACE on a plain press, you can always get back to MODS without touching the screen: press [[MENU]] on the play screen, or switch interface with the button next to LINK and press [[MENU]] there.

## LINK and the VJ.Tools view

When VJ.Tools on a computer is connected, the player's screen can show the VJ.Tools view instead of the play screen. MODS > CONNECT > VJ.TOOLS has a SCREEN SWITCH KEY with two choices:

| Choice | What switches the VJ.Tools view |
|---|---|
| LINK (default) | Pressing [[LINK]]. [[LINK]] also keeps its stock job. |
| ONSCREEN | A VJ.Tools button on the screen, in the corner opposite MODS. |

To switch the view with the button next to LINK instead, set that button to VJ.TOOLS SCREEN. See [Connect and DJ profiles](connect) for the connection itself.

7 @ 574,628 BEAT JUMP | Press for beat jump page 1. SHIFT + BEAT JUMP gives page 2.

| Key | Action with XZ Mods |
|---|---|
| [[SHIFT]] twice | Switches the left side between deck 1 and deck 3, or the right side between deck 2 and deck 4. Press it twice within about half a second, without touching anything else. Decks 3 and 4 need the loopback cable; without it the screen says DECK 3 AND 4 NEED THE CH3/CH4 ROUTE. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[SYNC]] | KEY SYNC: shifts the key of the deck this side controls to match the master deck, and turns on MASTER TEMPO. Press it again to end KEY SYNC. Works on all four decks. [[SYNC]] alone keeps its stock job on decks 1 and 2. |
| [[MASTER TEMPO]] | While KEY SYNC holds on that deck, a press ends KEY SYNC and returns the deck to its own key. Otherwise stock. [[SHIFT]] + [[MASTER TEMPO]] keeps its stock job. |
| [[HOT CUE]], [[BEAT LOOP]], [[SLIP LOOP]] | Choose the pad mode as on a stock player. The mode set in MODS > STEMS > STEM PADS turns four pads into stem pads. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[HOT CUE]] | Mutes or unmutes the first stem in your STEM ORDER (vocals by default) on that deck. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[BEAT LOOP]] | Mutes or unmutes the second stem (harmonics by default). |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[SLIP LOOP]] | Mutes or unmutes the third stem (drums by default). |
| [[BEAT JUMP]] | Beat jump mode with page 1 of your jump sizes. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[BEAT JUMP]] | Beat jump mode with page 2 of your jump sizes. Let go of [[SHIFT]] before you press a pad. |
| TEMPO slider | After a deck switch, the deck that takes over keeps its tempo until the slider crosses its value. Then the slider moves it again. This works both ways. |
| [[LOAD]] | Loads into the deck the side controls now. |
| Jog wheel | Stock on decks 1 and 2. On decks 3 and 4 it bends, scratches in VINYL mode and moves loop edges. The jog games use it too. |

The three SHIFT + pad mode combinations work while the USB loader has the stem engine. They also turn stem audio on if it was off. With them, the stock SHIFT + HOT CUE (CALL) is not available on decks 1 and 2.

Every other deck key keeps its stock job on decks 1 and 2. While a side drives deck 3 or 4, the transport, loop, cue and tempo keys play that software deck instead. [Four decks](four-decks) lists what each one does there.

## Performance pads

The eight pads keep their stock job in every pad mode, with two exceptions: the stem pads, and the BEAT JUMP pads.

| Pad mode | Pads with XZ Mods |
|---|---|
| HOT CUE | Stock hot cues. Gate cue and smart cue change what a hot cue does when you turn them on; see [Gate cue and smart cue](performance#gate-cue-and-smart-cue). |
| BEAT LOOP | Stock beat loops. |
| SLIP LOOP | Stock slip loops. |
| BEAT JUMP | Your jump sizes. A/B, C/D, E/F and G/H are pairs: the first pad jumps back, the second forward. With a loop on, the loop moves instead. [[SHIFT]] + pad keeps its stock job on decks 1 and 2. See [Beat jump](performance#beat-jump). |
| The stem mode | Four pads (A to D, or E to H) mute stems instead. The other four keep their job in that mode. |

### Stem pads

In the pad mode you choose for stems, four pads switch stems on that deck. A, B and C mute the three stems in your STEM ORDER. D is BYPASS: it plays the original track instead of the stems. On a 4-stem track, D mutes bass instead. With the E-H bank, E to H do the same.

```figure classic:theme-{look}-stem-pads MODS · STEM PADS
HOT CUE gives stem pads on A to D, in the default order, with stem colours on the pad lights.
1 @ 410,55 Pad mode | The pad mode button that gives stem pads. The others keep their pads.
2 @ 370,145 Pad diagram | What each pad does in that mode, laid out like the pads on the deck.
3 @ 572,143 STEMS ON | Which four pads carry the stems: PADS A-D or PADS E-H.
4 @ 706,206 Order | The order of the stems on pads A, B and C and on the play screen. Push, then turn to change it.
5 @ 712,286 Stem pad lights | STEM COLOURS lights stem pads in their stem colours and dims muted stems. OFF leaves the pad lights to the player.
6 @ 718,425 Works on | Stem pads work on decks 1 and 2. Decks 3 and 4 have no stems.
```

| Setting on this page | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Pad mode | HOT CUE, BEAT LOOP, SLIP LOOP, BEAT JUMP | HOT CUE |
| STEMS ON | PADS A-D, PADS E-H | PADS A-D |
| STEM PAD LIGHTS | STEM COLOURS, OFF | STEM COLOURS |

If you choose BEAT JUMP for stems, beat jumps win its pads. Page 2 always jumps. Page 1 jumps while it uses MY SIZES, and gives stem pads only while page 1 is set to STOCK. [Stems](stems) explains the stem audio itself, the order and the stem buttons on the screen.

### Pad lights

| What you see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Stem pads in stem colours, muted stems dim | STEM PAD LIGHTS is STEM COLOURS, on decks 1 and 2. |
| Warm red and amber pads in BEAT JUMP mode | Beat jump page 2 is selected on that deck. |
| Stock pad colours | Every other mode, and page 1 of BEAT JUMP. |

## Decks 3 and 4 from the deck controls

Each side of the XZ drives one of two decks: the left side deck 1 or deck 3, the right side deck 2 or deck 4. Press [[SHIFT]] twice to switch. [[LOAD]] on that side then loads into the deck it controls.

1. Connect the loopback cable and wait until MODS > 4 DECKS says the route is ready.
2. Press [[SHIFT]] twice quickly on the left side. The left side now drives deck 3.
3. Move the TEMPO slider until it reaches deck 3's tempo. Until then the slider does not change deck 3.
4. Press [[SHIFT]] twice again to go back to deck 1.

If the loopback route goes down, both sides go back to decks 1 and 2. [Four decks](four-decks) covers setup, the deck view and every status.

## When a control does not respond

| What happens | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| [[MENU]] opens a stock menu instead of MODS | That screen has its own MENU job. Press [[BACK]] to the play screen, then press [[MENU]]. |
| [[MENU]] opens UTILITY | You held it. A short press opens MODS. |
| The button next to LINK does nothing | It is set to NOTHING. Change it in MODS > PLAY > BUTTON NEXT TO LINK. |
| [[SHIFT]] twice shows DECK 3 AND 4 NEED THE CH3/CH4 ROUTE | Decks 3 and 4 are not connected. Open MODS > 4 DECKS. |
| The TEMPO slider does not change the deck | Pickup after a deck switch. Move the slider through the deck's tempo. |
| A key sync press shows a message on the deck's key | See [Keys and KEY SYNC](performance#keys-and-key-sync). |
| A BEAT JUMP pad shows JUMP UNAVAILABLE | See [When a jump is refused](performance#when-a-jump-is-refused). |
