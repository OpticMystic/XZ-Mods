XZ Mods leaves every control on the XDJ-XZ doing its normal job unless this page says otherwise. This page covers only the controls whose behaviour XZ Mods changes or adds, and the ones you use to drive MODS. For everything else, the XDJ-XZ operating instructions still apply.

## The button above LINK

The XDJ-XZ has a media button directly above [[LINK]]. Without XZ Mods it opens the library of a computer or phone connected to the player. XZ Mods gives it two slots: a plain press, and a press while [[SHIFT]] is held on either deck. Set them in MODS > PLAY > BUTTON ABOVE LINK.

| Choice | What the button does |
|---|---|
| MODS MENU | Opens and closes MODS. MODS then works with the rotary selector and [[BACK]]. |
| ITS OWN JOB | Its normal job: the screen for playing a computer's or phone's library. In MODERN the player's own screen for it takes over until you are back on the play screen. |
| INTERFACE | Switches between MODERN and CLASSIC, with the same notice as MODS > SCREEN. |
| VJ.TOOLS SCREEN | Switches the VJ.Tools screen on and off. Without an enabled, connected VJ.Tools link it says so instead. |
| STEMS | Does what the STEMS button on the screen does: steps the stem buttons through their places in 2 DECKS, and shows or hides them in MODERN's 4 DECKS. |
| DECK VIEW | Steps DECK VIEW: 2 DECKS, 4 DECKS in number order 1 2 3 4, 4 DECKS in mixer order 3 1 2 4, then 2 DECKS again. It saves the choice and shows the new view in a short notice. |
| NOTHING | The button does nothing. |

> **Tip.** With the default INTERFACE on a plain press, you can always get back to MODS without touching the screen: press [[MENU]] on the play screen, or switch interface with the button above [[LINK]] and press [[MENU]] there.

|---|---|

|---|---|
| [[SHIFT]] twice | Switches the left side between deck 1 and deck 3, or the right side between deck 2 and deck 4. Press it twice within about half a second, without touching anything else. Decks 3 and 4 need the loopback cable; without it the screen says DECK 3 AND 4 NEED THE CH3/CH4 ROUTE. |

| [[SHIFT]] + [[HOT CUE]] | Mutes or unmutes the first stem in your STEM ORDER (vocals by default) on that deck. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[BEAT LOOP]] | Mutes or unmutes the second stem (harmonics by default). |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[SLIP LOOP]] | Mutes or unmutes the third stem (drums by default). |
| [[BEAT JUMP]] | Beat jump mode with page 1 of your jump sizes. |
| [[SHIFT]] + [[BEAT JUMP]] | Beat jump mode with page 2 of your jump sizes. Let go of [[SHIFT]] before you press a pad. |
| TEMPO slider | After a deck switch, the deck that takes over keeps its tempo until the slider crosses its value. Then the slider moves it again. This works both ways. |
| [[LOAD]] | Loads into the deck the side controls now. |
| Jog wheel | Its normal job on decks 1 and 2. On decks 3 and 4 it bends, scratches in VINYL mode and moves loop edges. The jog games use it too. |

The three SHIFT + pad mode combinations work while the USB loader has the stem engine. They also turn stem audio on if it was off. With them, the player's own SHIFT + HOT CUE (CALL) is not available on decks 1 and 2.

Every other deck key keeps its normal job on decks 1 and 2. While a side drives deck 3 or 4, the transport, loop, cue and tempo keys play that deck instead. [Four decks](four-decks) lists what each one does there.

## Performance pads

The eight pads keep their normal job in every pad mode, with two exceptions: the stem pads, and the BEAT JUMP pads.

```nav physical-pad-mode-pages Performance pads
```

| Pad mode | Pads with XZ Mods |
|---|---|
| HOT CUE | The player's own hot cues. Gate cue and smart cue change what a hot cue does when you turn them on; see [Gate cue and smart cue](performance#gate-cue-and-smart-cue). |
| BEAT LOOP | The player's own beat loops. |
| SLIP LOOP | The player's own slip loops. |
| BEAT JUMP | Your jump sizes. A/B, C/D, E/F and G/H are pairs: the first pad jumps back, the second forward. With a loop on, the loop moves instead. [[SHIFT]] + pad keeps its normal job on decks 1 and 2. See [Beat jump](performance#beat-jump). |
| The stem mode | Four pads (A to D, or E to H) mute stems instead. The other four keep their job in that mode. |

### Stem pads

In the pad mode you choose for stems, four pads switch stems on that deck. A, B and C mute the three stems in your STEM ORDER. D is BYPASS: it plays the original track instead of the stems. On a 4-stem track, D mutes bass instead. With the E-H bank, E to H do the same.

## Decks 3 and 4 from the deck controls

Each side of the XZ drives one of two decks: the left side deck 1 or deck 3, the right side deck 2 or deck 4. Press [[SHIFT]] twice to switch. [[LOAD]] on that side then loads into the deck it controls.

```nav physical-double-shift-2 Decks 3 and 4 from the deck controls
```

1. Connect the loopback cable and wait until MODS > 4 DECKS says the route is ready.
2. Press [[SHIFT]] twice quickly on the left side. The left side now drives deck 3.
3. Move the TEMPO slider until it reaches deck 3's tempo. Until then the slider does not change deck 3.
4. Press [[SHIFT]] twice again to go back to deck 1.

If the loopback route goes down, both sides go back to decks 1 and 2. [Four decks](four-decks) covers setup, the deck view and every status.

## When a control does not respond

| What happens | Why, and what to do |
|---|---|
| [[MENU]] opens one of the player's own menus instead of MODS | That screen has its own MENU job. Press [[BACK]] to the play screen, then press [[MENU]]. |
| [[MENU]] opens UTILITY | You held it. A short press opens MODS. |
| The button above [[LINK]] does nothing | It is set to NOTHING. Change it in MODS > PLAY > BUTTON ABOVE LINK. |
| [[SHIFT]] twice shows DECK 3 AND 4 NEED THE CH3/CH4 ROUTE | Decks 3 and 4 are not connected. Open MODS > 4 DECKS. |
| The TEMPO slider does not change the deck | Pickup after a deck switch. Move the slider through the deck's tempo. |
| A key sync press shows a message on the deck's key | See [Keys and KEY SYNC](performance#keys-and-key-sync). |
| A BEAT JUMP pad shows JUMP UNAVAILABLE | See [When a jump is refused](performance#when-a-jump-is-refused). |
