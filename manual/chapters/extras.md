EXTRAS holds what is not about playing a set: games, apps from a USB, reporting a problem and the About page. Nothing here starts on its own. The XZ always starts in its normal DJ view, and a game or app opens only when you choose it.

Open MODS with [[MENU]], then choose **EXTRAS** in the section rail.

### Moving around a game

You do not need the touchscreen in any game.

| Control | In a game |
|---|---|
| Turn the encoder | Moves a ring over the controls on the screen |
| Push the encoder | Presses the control under the ring |
| [[BACK]] | Steps back |
| Touch | Presses what you touch. The ring hides until you turn the encoder again |

### Beat Arcade {experimental}

Turn the jog to move your paddle and tap **CUE** on each beat as the moving light meets the target. Good notes build charge. Eight good notes in a row earn a shield, and sixteen build a Drop that fires on the next downbeat.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Jog | Moves the paddle. In the menu, chooses a game |
| CUE or pad A | Strikes on the beat. Starts, resumes and rematches |
| Pad B | Wide paddle for 16 beats |
| Pad C | Saves one miss with a shield |
| Pad D | Queues a Drop on the next downbeat |
| Pads E and F | Aim the next return |
| Pad G | Changes the scene between Tunnel, Grid and Orbit |
| Pad H | Lines up with the mixer tempo, or taps a tempo |
| PLAY, track search | Work as usual on the music |
| **DJ VIEW** on screen | Saves the round and gives every control back to the deck |

Tap the source display at the top of the game to choose where the beat comes from: an analysed USB track, Pro DJ Link, the mixer tempo or four taps on pad H. For mixer tempo, set Beat FX to the channel you play, usually MASTER, and use AUTO BPM.

While Beat Arcade runs, touching the platter does not pause or scratch the music. Choose DJ VIEW before you go back to mixing.

### Wave Rider {experimental}

Choose **Solo**, **Co-op** or **Versus**, then the deck whose track you ride. In Solo that deck's jog steers. With two players, the left jog is player 1 and the right jog is player 2.

- Hold **CUE** or a top-row pad to add weight and gain speed downhill. Let go as the ground rises, and the crest throws you into the air.
- Turn the jog to tilt the board. Clockwise points the nose down.
- Land with the slope to keep your speed. Clean landings in a row score more.
- **EASY** is the default and keeps the angle you set in the air. **NORMAL** and **HARD** are stricter.

A missed landing never stops the music.

Each app row shows what kind of package it is and its state.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|

| RUNNING IN BACKGROUND | A service is running |
| APPLIED | A profile package is in use |
| FAILED | The app stopped. Push **RUN AGAIN** |
| BLOCKED | The package cannot run. Push **SET UP** to see how to fix it |
| CHECKING | The app manager is still reading the package |

## Report an issue

REPORT AN ISSUE sends the maintainer a problem, a question or an idea from the player. You say what it is about and, if you like, what happened. The player adds its own details. A topic and **SEND** take three presses.

1. **What's this about?** Push one of the five topics, or **NOT SURE**.
2. **What happened?** Type a few words, or push **SKIP, I'LL ADD IT ON MY COMPUTER**.
3. **Ready to send.** Check the summary, then push **SEND REPORT**.

With the encoder, push three times: the first topic, SKIP, then SEND.

### After you send

The report goes one of three ways. Whichever it is, nothing is lost and you never type it again.

| The screen says | What happened | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **SENT** and a reference like **R-4F7K-2M9Q** | It reached the XZ Mods inbox | Finish it on your computer: the XZ Mods app reminds you and shows the same reference |
| **SAVED ON THIS STICK** | The player is offline, its clock is not set, the loader is from an older XZ Mods app, or the inbox did not take it | Plug the stick into your computer. The XZ Mods app opens the report |
| **NOT SENT YET** | It could not be sent, and the stick could not take it | Do what the line says (put the stick back, use a writable stick, make room on it), then push **TRY AGAIN** |

When an error shows in the dock's top row, **REPORT THIS** beside it opens REPORT AN ISSUE at **WHAT HAPPENED?** with SOMETHING ISN'T WORKING chosen. Push **SKIP** and **SEND REPORT**, and the error goes with it. Every report includes an error on screen, however you open it.

### What the player sends

The report holds facts the player can check, never text read from its screens:

- the topic and your words, if any;
- the XZ Mods version and a fingerprint of your settings, with a few of them (stems, gate cue, interface, theme, deck view, browse preview);
- the loader ID from ABOUT and the ID of the computer that made the loader, so the report reaches your own XZ Mods app;
- the screen MODS was opened from (PLAY, BROWSE and so on), the interface, the deck view and the code of any error on screen;
- known status codes from the four-deck bridge and the player, and the recent entries of the USB diary on this stick.

It never holds music, track or playlist names, file names or network addresses. Only the maintainer reads it. You can also report from the XZ Mods app. See [Messages and troubleshooting](troubleshooting).
